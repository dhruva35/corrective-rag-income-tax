# Schedule XV: Eligible investments and payments for Section 123

This schedule specifies the investments and payments eligible for deduction under Section 123 of the Income Tax Act, 2025.

| Item No. | Eligible Investment / Payment | Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Life Insurance Premium | Must be paid to effect or keep in force an insurance on the life of the individual, spouse, or any child. Premium must not exceed 10% of the actual capital sum assured. |
| 2 | Public Provident Fund (PPF) | Contributions made to the PPF account of the individual, spouse, or child. |
| 3 | Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) | Contributions by an employee to a recognized provident fund. |
| 4 | Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) | Investment in units of an ELSS mutual fund. Subject to a lock-in period of 3 years. |
| 5 | National Savings Certificate (NSC) | Subscription to NSC VIII issue. |
| 6 | Home Loan Principal Repayment | Payment towards the cost of purchase or construction of a residential house property (principal amount only). |
| 7 | Tuition Fees | Payment of tuition fees to any university, college, school or other educational institution situated within India for the purpose of full-time education of any two children. |
