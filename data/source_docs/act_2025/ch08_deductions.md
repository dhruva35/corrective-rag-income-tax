# Chapter VIII: Deductions in respect of certain payments
## Part B — Deductions in respect of certain payments

### Section 123. Deduction for life insurance premia, deferred annuity, contributions to provident fund, etc.

(1) An individual or a Hindu undivided family, shall be allowed a deduction of the whole of the amount paid or deposited in the tax year, being the aggregate of the sums enumerated in Schedule XV, as does not exceed one lakh fifty thousand rupees, while computing the total income for that year, subject to the conditions specified in that Schedule.

(2) Where any amount is received by way of repayment under any of the schemes specified in Schedule XV, the amount so received shall be deemed to be income of the tax year in which it is received.

### Section 124. Deduction in respect of contribution to pension scheme of Central Government

(1) In the case of an individual, being an employee, who has paid or deposited any amount in his account under a pension scheme notified or as may be notified by the Central Government, he shall be allowed a deduction in the computation of his total income, of the whole of the amount so paid or deposited as does not exceed ten per cent of his salary in the tax year.

(1B) An assessee shall be allowed a deduction in computation of his total income, whether or not any deductions is allowed under sub-section (1), of the whole of the amount paid or deposited in the tax year in his account under a pension scheme notified by the Central Government, which shall not exceed fifty thousand rupees:
Provided that no deduction under this sub-section shall be allowed in respect of the amount on which a deduction has been claimed and allowed under sub-section (1).
